Scroll the Gurbani Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Scroll the Gurbani is made by Sandeep Singh Rai. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your reading stays on your phone.

What stays on your device

Your saved lines, likes, notes, comments, reading progress, streak, reminder times and settings are stored only on your iPhone. We don't have accounts and we don't receive this information.

Subscriptions

Scroll the Gurbani Plus is sold through Apple. Apple handles your payment, and we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history for this app and basic device information (such as iOS version and country). See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Reminders

Daily reminders and streak reminders are scheduled on your phone. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in iPhone Settings.

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, no selling of data, and no analytics profiles.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Deleting your data

Use Settings, then Reset all app data, or delete the app. Subscriptions are managed in your Apple ID settings.
